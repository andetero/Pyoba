About this program

Pyoba LLC, a Colorado company, builds and hosts simple websites for local businesses. We send text messages to business owners about their sample site, domain setup, site changes, and billing.

How you opt in

We collect consent verbally. When we call the number on a business’s public Google listing, we ask the owner, word for word:

"Can I text you the link and updates about your website at this number? Msg & data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime."

We only send texts after the owner says yes. You can also opt in by texting or emailing Pyoba first.

Confirmation message

After you opt in, we send this confirmation text:

"Pyoba: Thanks for agreeing to texts about your website. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Frequency, rates, and options

  • Message frequency varies (low volume).
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Support: support@pyoba.com

Privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for more.

Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
